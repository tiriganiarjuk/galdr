# Field audit — the three control surfaces

Generated by `probe/field_audit.py`. For each field: what its Verdandi
definition says it is for, what its name actually addresses, and where
those two disagree.

The surfaces were largely authored by agents that did not know what the
bundler and resolver would have to do with the names, so a plausible name
is not evidence that it works. The stated intent is the Verdandi field
definition; this compares the name against it.
